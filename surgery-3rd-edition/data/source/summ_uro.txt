Differential Diagnosis & Exclusion Criteria
Compare with: • Acute pyelonephritis (fever, positive culture, renal inflammation); • Musculoskeletal pain (localized tenderness, lack of urinary findings); • Ureteral stricture (chronic obstruction with imaging showing narrowing)
Differentiate from: • Pyelonephritis (imaging evidence of pelvis involvement, bacteremia); • Interstitial cystitis (chronic pain with negative cultures); • Prostatitis in men (PSA levels, treatment response)
Exclude: • Prostate cancer (hard, nodular gland, marked PSA elevation); • Chronic prostatitis (persistent pain with inflammatory markers); • Urethral stricture (focal narrowing on urodynamics/imaging)
Exclude: • BPH (diffuse, benign enlargement with modest PSA elevation); • Prostatitis (responds to antimicrobials; no malignant cells on biopsy)
Distinguish from: • Multicystic dysplastic kidney (nonfunctional, multiple isolated cysts on DMSA/IVU); • Simple renal cyst (well-defined, anechoic on CT/US); • Polycystic kidney disease (bilateral, familial pattern with numerous cysts)
Differentiate using imaging: • Renal contusion vs. laceration (CT defines capsule integrity); • Intraperitoneal vs. extraperitoneal bladder rupture (voiding cystogram leak pattern); • Anterior vs. posterior urethral injury (retrograde urethrogram shows location; pelvic fracture association)
